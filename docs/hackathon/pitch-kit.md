## 4. The 30-second pitch

> Most people never place a first trade because the screen asks them to read a chart. **Tagei replaces the chart with a rocket.** You pick LONG or SHORT, **hold** a button, and the rocket rides the real market for 30 seconds. When the round ends, it settles on BNB Chain from oracle prices recorded every second, so **every result has a receipt**. There is no wallet wall (Play as Guest is gasless), and our AI co-pilot explains the market but never predicts it. Pick a side. Hold. Ride.

## 5. The 60-second pitch

1. **Problem (10 s).** The first step into markets is a wall: wallets, gas, order types, charts, liquidation. Phones make it worse, and many crypto games hide how a result is produced.
2. **Insight (10 s).** The market itself can be the interface. A rocket that *is* the price is understood in one glance.
3. **Product (15 s).** Tap Play, tap a side, hold. Asset, stake and tier are preselected, so direction is the only decision. Hold to launch, hold to cash out. That is one gesture for the whole game.
4. **Trust (15 s).** Supra DORA-2 prices are recorded on BNB Chain once per second. Settlement replays those exact checkpoints, and the same evaluator runs in TypeScript and Solidity against shared test vectors. If data is missing, the stake is refunded.
5. **Close (10 s).** It is live on BNB Smart Chain Testnet today, with a gasless guest wallet, a non-predicting AI co-pilot and progression that rewards play, not stake size. *The market is the game.*

## 8. Judge Q&A cheat sheet

**Is this gambling?**
It is a short-round game on valueless test tokens today. We designed it to avoid the usual traps: a capped stake (5 to 50), no leverage and no liquidation, no auto-raised stakes, no loss-streak bonuses, no "win it back" copy, and XP that never scales with stake. A mainnet version would need its own legal review per jurisdiction.

**How is a result fair and verifiable?**
Entry is fixed 3 seconds after you commit, so nobody knows it when you press hold. Oracle prices are recorded on-chain each second. Settlement replays those exact checkpoints, and the same evaluator is tested in TypeScript and Solidity with shared golden vectors.

**What if the oracle stalls or a checkpoint is invalid?**
After 60 seconds of missing data, or on an invalid checkpoint, the round is voided and the full stake is refunded.

**Can the operator cheat?**
The operator cannot change a recorded checkpoint, because each one is verified on-chain. It could fail to record, which voids the round and refunds the stake. On testnet, one deployer key holds the admin roles. Mainnet would require a multisig and a timelock, and a second oracle path (a signed backup is already deployed but inactive).

**Why not just offer perps or binary options?**
Tagei is an *onboarding* experience, not a venue. The multiplier is a fixed payout, not leverage, and the loss is capped at the stake you chose.

**Why BNB Chain?**
A fresh on-chain round every 30 seconds, paid for by a relayer, needs a fast, low-fee chain. BNB Smart Chain also puts us next to an audience that already trades these assets.

**Why a rocket?**
Because up, down, target and stop are understood in one glance. The rocket does not sit on top of a chart. It is the chart.

**Is the guest wallet safe?**
It is built for testnet. The key lives in the browser and can be exported. A mainnet version would need stronger custody.

**Can PIX give bad advice?**
PIX is prompted to explain and never predict, its output is filtered for promise language, and it falls back to safe templates if anything slips through. It is also rate limited with a budget kill-switch.

**What is novel here?**
The combination: a one-gesture trade, per-second on-chain oracle checkpoints with a replayed settlement, and a product designed to avoid loss-chasing.

**What about the business model?**
The contracts include a house liquidity pool and a 1% fee on cash-out and timeout settlements. We have not modelled mainnet economics, and we say so.

**What is next?**
Higher tiers once lane economics are validated, a multisig and timelock plus a second oracle path, more markets, and quests and seasons.

## 9. Say this, not that

| Say | Do not say |
|---|---|
| "Testnet demo with valueless test tokens" | "Real money", "mainnet", "earn" |
| "Every result has a receipt" | "Provably fair" with no explanation, "unhackable" |
| "Internal security review" | "Audited" |
| "Settlement uses recorded checkpoints" | "Instant", "guaranteed" |
| "PIX explains the market" | "PIX tells you what to trade" |
| "Round complete" | "You lost", "failed" |
| "Hold to launch" | "Swipe up to trade" (it is only a hint, the entry is a button) |
| "CRUISE and BOOST tiers" | Promises about HYPER or WARP (switched off) |
| "Built for BNB Chain" | Win odds, house edge, expected returns |
